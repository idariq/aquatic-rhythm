Aquatic Rhythm Lab

Track your
tank's rhythm.

Where your tank actually is, one rhythm at a time.

This tool reads the tank you already have — not the one you're about to build. If there's no tank yet, Keeper Readiness Check is the better place to start: it only asks about you, and needs nothing running yet.

WaterChemistry read as a trend over time, not a single number
BiologicalMicrobial depth, and how well disruption is absorbed
EnvironmentalLight, flow, temperature, and layout as real inputs
LivestockBehaviour read as the earliest signal, ahead of parameters
KeeperYour own pattern of care — consistency, not perfection

Each rhythm can sit at a different phase at the same time — a filter can be mature while the lighting schedule is barely a week old. Check one now and get an answer immediately, or come back later and build toward the full picture. Nothing here needs to be done in order, and nothing here is being graded.

All five rhythms are ready below. What you get at the end is a reflection grounded in the ARA framework — not a diagnosis, and not a score to beat.
