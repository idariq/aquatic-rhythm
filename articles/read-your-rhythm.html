Read Your
Rhythm.

Where your tank actually is, one rhythm at a time.

This tool reads the tank you already have — not the one you're about to build. If there's no tank yet, Know Your Rhythm is the better place to start: it only asks about you, and needs nothing running yet.

ARA reads a tank through five rhythms — Water, Biological, Environmental, Livestock, and Keeper — and each one can sit at a different phase at the same time. A filter can be mature while the lighting schedule is barely a week old. Check one rhythm now and get an answer immediately, or come back later and build toward the full picture. Nothing here needs to be done in order, and nothing here is being graded.

Water Rhythm is ready below. The other four rhythms are still being built. What you get at the end is a reflection grounded in the ARA framework — not a diagnosis, and not a score to beat.
